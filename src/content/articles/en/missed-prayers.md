Few questions arrive at the imam desk more often, and few are answered with more guilt than they need. Here is the fiqh, plainly.

## Where the schools agree

The four schools agree that a prayer missed through sleep or forgetfulness is prayed when remembered, on the authority of the hadith: "Whoever sleeps through a prayer or forgets it, let them pray it when they remember it."

## Where they differ

They differ on prayers left deliberately: the majority hold that they are still made up alongside repentance, while a minority hold that the time cannot be recovered and repentance with abundant voluntary prayer is what remains.

## A plan that survives contact with real life

Pair each missed prayer with a current one — one make-up prayer after each of the five — and keep a written count. A small, steady rate finishes; an ambitious one stops in a week.
