After the death of Khadijah and Abu Talib in the same year, the Prophet ﷺ travelled to Taif to seek support and was driven out of the city.

## What he said afterwards

His du'a on that road contains no curse against the people who injured him. It begins with a complaint about his own weakness and ends with a statement of contentment: as long as Allah is not angry with him, he does not mind.

## The lesson

The seerah does not present this as a defeat converted into instant victory. Relief came years later. The lesson lives in the interval: what a believer says, and refuses to say, while the door is still closed.
