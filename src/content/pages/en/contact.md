## Write to us

Email: **info@imancorner.org**

We read everything, though a reply may take a few days.

## Corrections

If you have found a mistake in a translation, a grading or a ruling, please include the article title, the exact sentence, and your source. Corrections are published rather than quietly edited when they change the meaning.

## Events and contributions

Community groups can submit an event for the calendar, and writers can propose an article or a study series, using the same address.
