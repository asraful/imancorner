Iman Corner is a community project: a calendar of local events and programs, and a growing library of Quran, hadith, fiqh and seerah written for people with full lives.

## What we publish

Everything on this site falls into one of four topics — Quran, Hadith, Fiqh and Seerah — plus multi-part study series and the events calendar. Articles name their sources: the surah and verse, the collection and number, the school whose position is being described.

## How to reach us

Corrections are welcome and acted on. Write to us through the [contact page](/en/contact/) with the article, the sentence, and what you believe the correct reading to be.
