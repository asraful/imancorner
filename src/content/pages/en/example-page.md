This is a standalone page. It would be published at `/en/example-page/`.

Pages support full **markdown**: headings, lists, links, images uploaded through
the CMS, and so on.

Turn on _Show in menu_ to add the page to the site's header navigation; _Menu
order_ controls its position there. Use the language switcher at the top of the
editor to write the Arabic and Finnish versions of the page — all translations
are edited together in this one entry, exactly like events.
