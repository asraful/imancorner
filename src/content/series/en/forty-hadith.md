Imam an-Nawawi gathered forty-two narrations that, between them, carry the axes of the religion. This series takes one per session: the Arabic text, a plain translation, the grading, and what the narration asks of an ordinary week.

Each part is short by design. Read one, sit with it, and come back next week rather than finishing the collection in a weekend and remembering none of it.
